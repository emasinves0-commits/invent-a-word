A make.fun entry for [Invent a Word We Need](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-invent-a-word-we-need-17a020e0).

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network) writes one invented English word with its one-line definition (72 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> bloodlaugh — the moment your father's laugh comes out of your own mouth.

The word names a gap English has never filled: the uncanny instant a parent's laugh surfaces in your own throat. It needs no bereavement to land — nearly every reader has a parent and has felt it — and "blood" turns from gore to heredity (bloodline, bellylaugh), so the coinage sounds like English grew it.
